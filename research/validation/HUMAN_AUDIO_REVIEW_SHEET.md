## Uniform review items

Every row requires all ten checks over the complete listed envelope:

- `U1` — dialogue overlap;
- `U2` — silence or near-silence;
- `U3` — ambience and room/exterior tone;
- `U4` — score entrance and exit;
- `U5` — sound before picture;
- `U6` — off-screen sound;
- `U7` — sound bridge across an edit or visible state change;
- `U8` — subjective sound or an altered listening perspective;
- `U9` — object, body, vehicle, machinery, or environmental sound;
- `U10` — the exact audible moment at which audience information changes.

For each item, record `PRESENT`, `ABSENT`, or `UNKNOWN`. `ABSENT` requires listening to the complete envelope; an uncertain or masked event remains `UNKNOWN`.

## How to record a completed audition

A row remains blocked until the complete envelope has been listened to and the result is written back as reproducible evidence. For each completed row:

1. Add a versioned direct-audition method record containing a method ID, review date, playback speed, selected audio track/channel handling, playback route, and whether video was viewed in sync. Do not record a private media path or reviewer personal data.
2. For every positive claim, record `AUDIO_OBSERVED`, the Evidence ID, the smallest relevant Shot ID set, and exact source-local start and end timecodes. Describe only the audible event, its audible on-screen/off-screen/unknown source status, and its observed relation to picture or an edit.
3. Record `PRESENT`, `ABSENT`, or `UNKNOWN` for `U1–U10`. Anything masked, ambiguous, outside the envelope, not independently heard, or supported only by subtitles/scripts remains `UNKNOWN`.
4. Keep interpretation separate as `INFERRED`. Audible adjacency alone does not prove shared trigger, reaction cause, chronology, identity, relationship, motive, production method, or edit intention.
5. For `SOUND-OF-METAL-SIGNAL-STATE-EE-V0.1`, cite decoded-signal measurements only as separate auxiliary evidence. Do not use them to name, explain, or confirm an audible semantic event.
6. Re-run the Scene Evidence validator after recording audio claims. A passing structure check is not human creative approval or permission to promote an audio rule.

Until those records exist, none of these 31 evidence units may emit a directly observed semantic audio rule.
