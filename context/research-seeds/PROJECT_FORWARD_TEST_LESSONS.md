# Private Project Forward-Test Lessons

The underlying 36-episode scripts and Director IR are private and are not included in this public repository. This document preserves only reusable system findings.

## Verified structural findings

- A locked script can be compiled into a model-neutral IR while preserving dialogue order, reveal order, source-beat coverage, and cross-episode continuity.
- Static coverage is a useful AI default, but the scene still needs rhythm changes through scale, POV, reaction priority, and motivated cuts.
- Every high-risk shot needs a typed fallback, not a vague note to simplify later.
- Project-original Scene Masters and Shot Golden Frames are safer continuity anchors than reference-film stills.
- Visible text, controlled shadows, liquids, wetness, and precise surface reflections should be assigned to explicit composite layers when one-pass generation is unreliable.
- A child or other protected participant leaving a scene may require an explicit visual/audio boundary before adult intimacy or violence changes state.
- Structural validation proves contract completeness; it does not prove the result is well directed, visually accepted, or authorized for generation.

## Golden Case coverage

The two private cases tested:

1. dialogue-driven misdirection, prop evidence, threshold blocking, and a restrained supernatural reveal;
2. three-character compressed staging, emotional refusal, mundane physical interruption, and state continuity across wetness/composite layers.

They did not test pursuit, combat, large crowds, complex geography, musical staging, montage, extended horror, or multi-location procedural sequences. Those remain research gaps.
